Jem Social — scaling content with a 100-page programmatic build.

A structured Webflow CMS build designed to generate and manage 100 SEO-targeted pages without manual page-building.

Platform

Webflow CMS

Scale

100 pages

Focus

Programmatic SEO

The brief

Jem Social needed a large set of SEO-targeted landing pages built out fast — the kind of page volume that isn't practical to design and publish one at a time. The goal was a system, not a hundred individual pages.

The approach

Rather than build each page manually, the site was structured around a Webflow CMS Collection: one flexible page template, driven by structured content fields, so each of the 100 pages could be generated consistently — same design system, same SEO structure, different content per page.

The result

A scalable content system that let Jem Social publish 100 pages in a fraction of the time a manual build would take, with consistent structure and on-page SEO across every page in the collection.
